GOOD TO KNOW

A few friendly answers.

Can I come on my own?+

Absolutely. Many friendships start with a simple hello. Our hosts will help you find a table.

Do I need to know how to play?+

Not at all. There will be familiar games and people ready to explain the rules.

Do I need to belong to a church?+

No. Summit Life Church and Rock of Ages Church are hosting, and everyone in our community is welcome.

What if I cannot make it?+

Use your private RSVP link to cancel. It helps us plan the food and makes room for someone on the waitlist.

Can I bring the kids?+

Yes. Include them when you RSVP so we can plan the seats and pizza. Children must stay with a responsible adult.

What if the night is full?+

Join the waitlist. When space opens, your group gets a limited-time offer on your private RSVP page. Text updates are optional when available.
